# 417 Local: the big 7 design fixes (Nov 25, 2026)

Source: side-by-side screenshots of the Vercel preview and the live WordPress site (1280 px and 390 px). Screenshots live in the FF Marketing Architect project files at `417-design/shots/`.

Preview: https://417-local-website-josh-lenzs-projects.vercel.app

## Ranked

1. **Event rows are an undifferentiated wall.** Every row is the same blue underlined link on the same grey date chip. Times show on under half of rows, price on almost none, and some "venues" are street addresses (e.g. "1570 E Battlefield Ste. E, Springfield"). A visitor can't tell what is tonight, free, or worth the drive. Fix: day jump bar (Fri/Sat/Sun), time as a visible left column or "Time TBA" chip, Free chip, venue never a raw address, category chips become filters.
2. **/events/ lost its hero and its email signup.** Old page: photo, "Get all new experiences delivered right to your inbox", email box, then a calendar. New page: flat blue band and four boxes. We lost the only lead capture on the busiest page. Fix: restore a hero with a photo and an email signup (same destination as the old form; confirm with Josh).
3. **City cards on /events/ have no hierarchy.** Springfield has a giant "115" and three bold underlined links; the others have one line. The "This weekend" button is the keyword page and should be the loudest thing on the card. Fix: one featured event, one primary button ("This weekend"), a small count, no underlined link stack.
4. **Junk and noise in the headline slots.** "Black Friday Bargains", emoji titles ("BLACK FRIDAY" with cart emoji), store sales and all-caps titles show as the top pick for a city. Fix: title cleanup (strip emoji, fix ALL CAPS), retail-sale filter, and never choose a "top pick" without a time and a real venue. Touches event data, so coordinate with the 417 Local Architect.
5. **Weekend page has no hero, no photo, and nothing that says "weekend".** `/things-to-do-in-springfield-mo-this-weekend/` already exists (and for Branson, Joplin, Lebanon, Bolivar). It reads like a data export under a plain blue band. Fix: a real hero (photo plus the dates "Fri Nov 27 to Sun Nov 29"), "Top 5 picks" cards with images above the full list, and an "also this weekend" list below.
6. **Things-to-do guide is a 10,000 px wall.** Desktop is about 10,300 px tall and mobile about 19,000 px. Same content as the old page, but no table of contents, no sticky jump links, and the first screen has no images. Fix: sticky "On this page" jump bar, an image-led top 10 before the long text.
7. **Home page is quiet and sprawling.** Old hero (fountain) is kept, but there is no event module at all. Events is a text section with three buttons at the bottom of the page, even though events is the main reason to come back weekly. Fix: add "This weekend in Springfield" (3 cards) right under the hero, and a single email signup band.
