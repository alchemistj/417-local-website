## The scoop

This ladies only event will take place at the beautiful Timber Line Barn. The venue is usually used for wedding events, but will be transformed into the 90s prom of your dreams for just one magical night.

[img: Classy dinner table]

Your $25 ticket to the Mom Prom will include a catered dinner, of course dancing, a cash bar, a silent auction, a photo booth, and more. **[CHECK: $25 price and inclusions are 2021 figures]**

I’m totally stoked!!!

## Do it for the kids

Not only will this be an absolutely fantastic night out with the girls, but you’ll also be giving to a great cause.

The money raised will be going to the Beau Fournier Fund benefiting the Buffalo MO School Supply Drive.

Beau’s mother started the School Supply Drive **in 2020** as a way to honor the memory of her son and the generous spirit that always shined through him.

Sponsors have gathered together to make Mom Prom possible and create an outlet for you to join in on the generosity.
