## 1. The starting point

- A Springfield, Missouri things-to-do site on old WordPress with The Events Calendar plugin.
- 54 posts, 22 pages, 932 old event URLs. Recurring events last updated 2021 to 2022.
- Heavy impressions in search, but no updates in years.
- Why we own it: a real site we can test on without risking a client.
- Baseline figures: [GSC: total impressions and clicks, last 90 days], [GSC: top 20 pages by impressions], [PageSpeed: mobile score].

## 2. What we changed

- Rebuilt on Astro. Faster pages, clean URLs.
- Events hubs for Springfield, Joplin, Lebanon, Bolivar and Branson, fed daily from official event sources.
- Old posts sorted three ways: evergreen (kept, refreshed), annual (original writer's words, dates refreshed yearly), past one-offs (redirected or retired).
- 932 old event URLs redirected to city and category hubs.
- Structure for AI answer engines (GEO): clear page headings, plain-language event facts, "how we pick" page, `llms.txt`. [Confirm exactly what shipped before writing.]
- Event submission and contact forms through Central Forms.

## 6. What clients can take from it

- Check what the old site already earns before you move anything. Keep what works.
- Redirect carefully and test every redirect.
- Fresh, specific, checkable facts do better than filler. [Support with results.]

- Test one change at a time.
