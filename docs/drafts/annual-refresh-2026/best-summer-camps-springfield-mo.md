# 19+ Spectacular Summer Camps in Springfield MO Area

May 25, 2022

By 417 Local Editorial Team

**2027 update: Cub Creek Science Camp and YMCA Camp Wakonda have posted their 2027 dates and rates, The Summit says 2027 registration opens in early winter, and most other camps below still show 2026 details, so check each camp's page for current dates and prices.**

Help your child pass the long days of summer while giving them magical memories at any of these amazing summer camps around the area.

Whatever their interest, there is definitely a great option for them. Here you will find wonderful camps for your athlete, artist, dancer, tech wiz, actor, animal lover, scientist, crafter, and more! 

## AR Workshop

[[img: ar workshop springfield mo]](https://www.arworkshop.com/new-releases-for-summer-camp/)

Looking for some artsy crafty fun?

[Anders Ruff®](https://andersruff.com/custom-printable-parties/), a popular graphic design, party fashion, DIY blog, and online store, was founded in 2010 by Maureen (Anders) and Adria (Ruff).

In 2016 they had a desire to begin teaching locally and birthed the AR Workshop at [1550 E. Battlefield in Springfield MO](https://www.google.com/maps/place/AR+Workshop+Springfield/@37.1471163,-93.2928896,12z/data=!3m1!5s0x87cf64b130a959a3:0x7660d443c9dbd2c!4m8!1m2!2m1!1sar+workshop!3m4!1s0x87cf64a580dccce3:0x958bc7844c3bc29!8m2!3d37.1587959!4d-93.2689638).

They offer [multiple Summer Camp options ](https://www.arworkshop.com/new-releases-for-summer-camp/)in tie-dye, abstract photo frame, rock cactus pot, mini plank tray, canvas pillow, and paint and string art, chunky yarn wreath, mini plank tray, canvas wall hanging.

You can check out the AR Workshops [Springfield schedule here](https://www.arworkshop.com/springfield/) or call [(417) 429-0803](tel:(417) 429-0803).

Cost: $185

## Cub Creek Science Camp

If you are looking for science, animal, or a traditional overnight summer camp for your adventurer, [Cub Creek Science Camp](https://cubcreeksciencecamp.com/) is the place for you!

Their camp is full of fun, including hundreds of animals and activity choices like rope courses, survival skills, and so much more!

Jam-packed with enjoyable activities your camper age 7-17 is sure to have the time of their life!

Cost: ~~$1095~~ **$1695 per week**

## Dance Mission

[[img: dance mission summer camp]](https://www.dancemission.org/summer-camps)

Do you have a little one full of expressive energy? 

[Dance Mission near Fremont and Republic Rd in Springfield](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Dance+Mission/@37.1352345,-93.2730467,17z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m5!3m4!1s0x87cf64c5278a6fa7:0xa7af469d05de0b6a!8m2!3d37.1352345!4d-93.270858) believes in teaching students not only how to be a phenomenal dancer, acrobat and aerialist. But they also teach their students perspective on life, building character and the ability to give back. 

These special [2-day camps at Dance Mission](https://www.dancemission.org/summer-camps) will empower your children beyond the studio floor learning about motivation, responsibility, and being a phenomenal individual who displays their brilliant confidence.

Choose from 3 different summer camps with different skills emphasis:

Dance and plant for ages 4-7 and 8-13 

Dance and paint for ages 3-6 and 7-12

Circus for ages 7-9 and 10-13

Each class has an ending performance for family and friends and you can learn more from their website above or by calling [(417) 894-1187](tel:(417) 894-1187).

Cost: **check Dance Mission’s camps page for current pricing**
