# 12 Strawberry, Blueberry & Blackberry Picking Farms in 417 [2022] **[CHECK: '12' and '[2022]' left as published: the post lists 11 farms, and only Iron Gate has posted 2027 season dates; editor to decide the count and year at publish]**

May 5, 2022

By Carla Broderick

**2027 update: Missouri Berries is now under new ownership and Iron Gate Berry Farm has posted its 2027 picking dates; most other farms below had not posted 2027 details as of Nov. 2026, so check each farm's page before you go.**

Strawberry fields forever.

Ok. Maybe not forever.

But definitely in the summer and definitely all around 417 land!!!

Oh, and also blueberries, and blackberries, and raspberries. **[CHECK: raspberries: IronGate no longer lists them and none of the other farms' readable sites mention raspberries; confirm any farm still offers them]**

Here are some of the best farms to go berry picking in our gorgeous area:

## Missouri Berries

[2097 S State Hwy PP Republic, MO 65738](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=37.17832%2C-93.57641&fbclid=IwAR3QI5aqFOWBir2qDUVA0VYhAtRFFfAZu3QMKRL1IHrTcfDWYnja6GYsfHM)

**Under new ownership is Missouri Berries!**

After years of learning the ropes, the Rogers family of four **opened** their welcoming farm to the community, **and it is now owned by Randy and Johnelle Little.** ~~And this year they have 50,000 strawberry plants ready for you!~~ **The farm has strawberry, blueberry and blackberry fields ready for you!** Stop buy to pick some perfectly ripened strawberries, stay for the fresh fruit donuts, slushies, and plenty of outdoor fun on their 40 acre farm. **[CHECK: '40 acre farm' and 'fresh fruit donuts, slushies' are not on missouriberries.com (its FAQ lists a 4 acre strawberry field, 6 acre blueberry field, 2 acre blackberry field and says 'The concession trailer will be open on the weekends'); the old '50,000 strawberry plants' line was struck because the About page says the Rogers family planted 'over 6,000 blueberry bushes and 25,000 strawberry plants'; no 2027 dates posted, site says 'open based on the weather and field ripeness' and points to Facebook]**

Keep up with their adventure [here](https://www.missouriberries.com/).

## Peace Lane Berry Farm **[CHECK: only a Facebook page is linked and no official website was found; Facebook pages return a login wall to curl, so open/closed status, picking dates and 2027 season could not be confirmed]**

[9262 E Peace Ln, Strafford, MO 65757](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Peace+Lane+Berry+Farm/@37.2034544,-93.0919155,15z/data=!4m5!3m4!1s0x0:0xc928711693816ccc!8m2!3d37.2034544!4d-93.0919155)

This farm throws all of their talents into growing delicious blueberries. They’re usually ready for you mid June and go until July. The farm makes sure to use organic fertilizers and produce large sweet blueberries ready for eating or throwing in your favorite cobbler.

They post all of their hours and info [here](https://www.facebook.com/peacelaneberry/?ref=page_internal).

## Horton’s Berry Farm **[CHECK: only a Facebook page is linked and no official website was found; Facebook pages return a login wall to curl, so open/closed status, picking dates and 2027 season could not be confirmed]**

[2385 HWY DD, Marshfield, MO 65706](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Horton's+Berry+Farm/@37.3385775,-92.8523545,17z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m5!3m4!1s0x87c56110320c1b8b:0xd5fde37d91452f71!8m2!3d37.3385775!4d-92.8501658)

Horton’s is family owned and operated. They bring you the trifecta of berries for picking: strawberries, blueberries, and blackberries. I can already picture pilling all three freshly picked berries right on top of a scoop of Andy’s custard. They’ve been hard at work getting ready for the berry picking season by planting over 15,000 plants- and that’s just the strawberries!

When the berries are ripe for the picking, they’ll post their hours [here](https://www.facebook.com/Hortons-Berry-Farm-115042036917604/).

## Brown’s Berry Farm **[CHECK: only a Facebook page is linked and no official website was found; Facebook pages return a login wall to curl, so open/closed status, picking dates and 2027 season could not be confirmed]**

[img: ]

[5935 State Hwy M, Miller, MO 65707](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Brown's+Berry+Farm/@37.1906332,-93.7347129,17z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m5!3m4!1s0x87cf54d9f6f9c5f3:0xfeb8682ae6bfe665!8m2!3d37.1906332!4d-93.7325242)

## IronGate Berry Farm

[16225 Johnson Rd, Eldridge, MO 65463](https://www.google.com/maps/place/IronGate+Berry+Farm/@37.801117,-92.7408449,15z/data=!4m5!3m4!1s0x0:0xa97ba0c5d191f673!8m2!3d37.801117!4d-92.7408449)

**Three, that’s right, THREE** different berries will be available for you to treat yourself with at this farm! IronGate Berry Farm is growing strawberries, blackberries, **and** blueberries. They’ll open up their farm to you for picking in **May (2027: strawberries May through June, blackberries mid-June through July, blueberries late June through July)** and will continue to invite you over until all the picking is done (usually July). You’ll also be able to grab some local honey and fresh seasonal produce when you visit their farm. **[CHECK: local honey and seasonal produce are not mentioned on irongateberryfarm.com; the site also says U-pick sessions are reserved online and to 'always call before you go', (417) 426-5341]** Get all the info [here](https://irongateberryfarm.com/).

[5313 S Farm Rd 241, Rogersville, MO 65742](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Black+and+Blue+Berry+Farm/@37.1126951,-93.1018447,15z/data=!4m5!3m4!1s0x0:0x2e5d2677ba9afd80!8m2!3d37.1126951!4d-93.1018447)

This urban farm uses only natural fertilizers to produce the blueberries. I know it’s outside of the berry realm, but I also wanted to mention they offer lavender for you to pick! How fun is that! Be sure to make a day out of this farm visit because they also have tea to sip, cinnamon rolls to taste, ice cream to enjoy, and acres of property to explore. Find out more about this amazing farm [here](https://www.facebook.com/blackandblueberryfarm).

## Persimmon Hill Farm

[img: ]

[367 Persimmon Hill Ln, Lampe, MO 65681](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Persimmon+Hill+Farm/@36.532793,-93.5037286,17z/data=!4m8!1m2!2m1!1spersimmon+hill+farm!3m4!1s0x87ced8cb5ae28311:0x83d4bf15bd76da6a!8m2!3d36.5338836!4d-93.5019321)

Their farm also offers a plethora of berry filled baked goods, jams, and syrups. Once you’ve burned all those calories picking the berries, replenish your energy by stopping by their farm-to-table restaurant situated right next to the blueberry fields. ## Hummingbird Berry Farm

[5581 S 140th Rd, Brighton, MO 65617](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Hummingbird+Berry+Farm/@37.441105,-93.3836215,17z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m5!3m4!1s0x87c5928ad47de4e7:0x5e6a849c50e8e635!8m2!3d37.441105!4d-93.3814328)

Blackberries are this farm’s specialty and they know how to do it right. Hummingbird Berry Farm has 3 different varieties of blackberries that are grown thornless and on trellises for easy picking. As their name implies, Hummingbird Berry Farm also provides plenty of homemade nectar in feeders placed throughout the farm to attract hummingbirds. So expect to see a few of those cuties buzzing by as you peruse the farm. Find all their info [here](https://hummingbirdberryfarm.com/). **[CHECK: hummingbirdberryfarm.com answers HTTP 200 but is a JavaScript-only page; curl sees only the title 'Home' and no text, so hours, varieties and 2027 season could not be confirmed]**

### Quick! Go Pick!

It’s obvious that 417 land is a prime spot to grow some great berries. You have quite a few farms to choose from when you’re ready to venture out. The picking season will go by quickly, so don’t wait too long to pick a farm, pick a date, and pick a berry. Whatever place you go to get your berry picking on, I know you’re going to have a berry good time.
