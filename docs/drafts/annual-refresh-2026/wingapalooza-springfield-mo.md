## What to expect

Wingapalooza is a wing tasting event. Restaurants and businesses from around the Springfield area set up booths and hand out samples, and compete for the event’s awards, including a Facebook Choice Award voted on by the public. Go hungry.

Expect a crowd. Plan on lines at the popular booths. There is live music and a wing eating contest during the day; the schedule is on the official site.

**In 2026, the night before (Friday, November 6),** Sertoma also **hosted** a Texas Hold ‘Em poker tournament with live music at the Expo Center. Details are on the official site. **[CHECK: 2027 poker night not confirmed]**

## Tickets

Advance tickets are $25 online through [wingapalooza.com](https://wingapalooza.com), or $30 at the door on the day. You can also buy them at Hy-Vee and Rapid Robert’s stores. **[CHECK: 2026 prices and sales outlets; confirm for the next event]**

## Tips for going

- Buy ahead. It saves $5 and you skip the ticket line.

- Eat light beforehand and wear something you do not mind getting sauce on.

- Bring wet wipes. Napkins run out.

- Get there early if you want the shortest lines.

## Where the money goes

Sertoma runs Wingapalooza as a fundraiser. The Springfield Sertoma Clubs have supported the Boys & Girls Clubs of Springfield since 1941 and focus on at-risk youth in southwest Missouri. So the wings are for a good cause.

## A bit of history

Wingapalooza is a long-running Springfield tradition. In 2020, Sertoma ran it as the Wingapalooza Crawl instead of one big event. That version used a booklet of vouchers you could redeem at participating restaurants through the end of the year. The big gathering is back but details can change. Always confirm times, prices and rules on the [official Wingapalooza site](https://wingapalooza.com) before you go.
