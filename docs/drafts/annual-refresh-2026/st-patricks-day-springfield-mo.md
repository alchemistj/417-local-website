# 10 St. Patrick’s Day Celebrations That Make You Feel Lucky to Live in 417 Land (and one extra)

March 8, 2022

By Carla Broderick

**2027 update: this roundup was written for 2022, and the organizers haven't posted 2027 dates yet, so each date below is marked to be confirmed.**

Everyone’s a little Irish on St. Patrick’s Day right?

Whether you’re a wee lad or an ol lass, you’ll find some Celtic fun right here in and around Springfield MO!

Note:

These events are more or less in order of their occurrence.

Let’s dive in.

## St. Patrick’s Clay Creating

###### Art Zone

**[Date to be confirmed: the organizer hasn't posted 2027 dates yet]**

Hone your Irish pride into an artful creation!

The Art Zone will be offering options like pot-o-gold, leprechaun hat vases, and shamrock photo holders.

You can stay at the studio to create your masterpiece or you can take a Kit To Go.

This activity is for all ages, so bring the whole fam!

Get all the info [here](https://www.artzonenixa.com/event-details/st-patricks-clay).

**[Date to be confirmed: the organizer hasn't posted 2027 dates yet]**

###### Fleet Feet Springfield

**[Date to be confirmed: the organizer hasn't posted 2027 dates yet]**

So much St. Patrick’s Day fun happening at Fleet Feet!!

**[Date to be confirmed: the organizer hasn't posted 2027 dates yet]**
