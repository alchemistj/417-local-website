# 10 St. Patrick’s Day Celebrations That Make You Feel Lucky to Live in 417 Land (and one extra)

March 8, 2022

By Carla Broderick

**Update: Fleet Feet’s Shamrock Shuffle is Saturday, March 13, 2027, in downtown Springfield, so check each event’s site for this year’s dates before you go.**

Everyone’s a little Irish on St. Patrick’s Day right?

Whether you’re a wee lad or an ol lass, you’ll find some Celtic fun right here in and around Springfield MO!

Note:

These events are more or less in order of their occurrence.

Let’s dive in.

## St. Patrick’s Clay Creating

###### Art Zone

**Check the organizer’s site for this year’s date**

Hone your Irish pride into an artful creation!

The Art Zone will be offering options like pot-o-gold, leprechaun hat vases, and shamrock photo holders.

You can stay at the studio to create your masterpiece or you can take a Kit To Go.

This activity is for all ages, so bring the whole fam!


## Conservatory of the Ozarks St. Patrick’s Recital

###### The Creamery Arts Center

**Check the organizer’s site for this year’s date**

Looking for a more relaxing way to celebrate St. Patrick?

The Conservatory is holding a recital full of Celtic and Irish inspired music for all to enjoy.

If you’d like more info about this event, feel free to contact Conservatory of the Ozarks at 417-592-1756 or email them at [ConservatoryoftheOzarks@gmail.com](mailto:ConservatoryoftheOzarks@gmail.com)

## Art Camp: Luck of the Irish

###### Art Zone

**Check the organizer’s site for this year’s date**

Let your child spend the whole day at Irish themed art camp!

They will start the morning with painting, break for lunch, and then do some fun sculpture work for the second half of the day.

This art camp welcomes kiddos 5 years and older.


## Shamrock Shuffle Presented By Fleet Feet Springfield

###### Fleet Feet Springfield

**Saturday, March 13, 2027**

So much St. Patrick’s Day fun happening at Fleet Feet!!

The day starts with some races! There will be a 15k, a 5k, and of course, a beer mile.

**Everyone who runs gets a medal, and the 15k starts at 8am, the 5k at 8:10am and the beer mile at 10am, all in downtown Springfield.**

Get ready to party Irish style and grab all the info [here](https://runsignup.com/Race/MO/Springfield/ShamrocksandShenanigansFleetFeetSpringfield).

## St. Patrick’s Day Celebration

###### Country Roads Family Fun Farm

**Check the organizer’s site for this year’s date**

If you’re looking for a St. Patrick’s Day celebration for the whole family, head on over to Country Roads Farm!

Along with their normal fun activities like the petting zoo, farm rides, and play area, Country Roads is adding some Irish themed shenanigans like St. Patrick’s Day crafts and a pot-o-gold treasure hunt!

For more info and ticket pricing check out their site [here](https://country-roads-family-fun-farm-llc.square.site/product/St.Patricks_Day_Celebration/482).

## St Patrick’s Bike Ride

###### Springbike Bicycle Club

**Check the organizer’s site for this year’s date**

Put on your best green riding outfit and come join the fun at the annual St. Patrick’s Bike Ride!

Your group will start at James River Church parking lot and have the option for 24 or 33 mile long rides.

Want to spend even more time riding? You can also opt for a 50 mile ride that starts at Dan Kinney park!

Go to their website [here](https://springbike.org/rides/calendar/) for all the info.

## Lucky’s St. Patrick’s Day Crawl

###### Downtown Springfield

**Check the organizer’s site for this year’s date**

Get out and have some fun like any true Irish – with beer!

- Check in at the starting pub and start the crawl off right with a complimentary drink or shot.

- Continue on to participating pubs and get exclusive drink specials and amazing food.

- End the night with an epic after party at the final pub!

There will be a costume contest! So hone in on all that Irish luck and flaunt your best green gear!


## St Patty’s at Springfield Brewing Company

###### **Check the organizer’s site for this year’s date**

SBC **partied** all week long!

**In 2022** you **could** stop by and rock out at their Ozark Mountain Daredevil afterparties!! There **was** live music (from friends and fellow musicians of the band) and Ozark Mountain Daredevil gin for pour and for purchase.

Things will get especially Irish on March 17th will food and beer specials all day long!

Follow their Facebook page [here](https://www.facebook.com/Springfieldbrewingco) as more details emerge.

## Dublin’s Pass Downtown

###### March 17th

Of course, one of the best Irish pubs in town is going to be celebrating it up big for St. Patty’s!

All day long they’ll be serving up traditional Irish dishes like corned beef and cabbage and pouring out green beer. There will also be some other great specials waiting for you when you arrive!

Check out all they have to offer [here](https://www.dublinspass.com/home).

## Finnegan’s Wake

###### **Check the organizer’s site for this year’s date**

With a name like Finnegan’s Wake you know they know how to throw the best Irish party around.

The festivities will kick off with their Drunken Bruncheon.

Come back later that night to join them in the Downtown St. Patrick’s Pub Crawl.

Then on St. Patrick’s Day, they’ll be throwing another Drunken Bruncheon and keep the party going all day with live music, green beer, Irish whiskey, and all sorts of fun!

Keep up with all of their shenanigans [here](https://www.facebook.com/finneganssgf/).

## St. Patrick’s Day Parade – Eureka Springs

###### Eureka Springs, Arkansas

**Check the organizer’s site for this year’s date**

**Check with the Downtown Springfield Association to see whether 417 land’s own St. Patrick’s Day Parade is scheduled this year.**

Fortunately, Eureka Springs isn’t too far of a drive and they have all the parade festivities to fill your Irish lovin heart!

Their parade **starts at the Eureka Springs Library and winds through town** (details can be found [here](https://kaptains.org/index.php/paradeinfo/)).

Make sure you leave some time to hang out in the town after the parade too! They know how to do St. Patrick’s Day right with live music, great food, and blarney fun!
