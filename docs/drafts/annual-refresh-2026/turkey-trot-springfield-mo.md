# Turkey Trot in Springfield is Still On! Here’s How…

November 24, 2020

By Carla Broderick

**2026 update: The 32nd Annual Thanksgiving Turkey Trot 5K is Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, at 8 a.m. in downtown Springfield; details are at [ParkBoard.org/TurkeyTrot](https://www.parkboard.org/TurkeyTrot).**

Does running entice you? Do you get a thrill from pounding pavement in the painfully cold mornings of late fall? Does the thought of helping a good cause while getting a free t-shirt bring a smile to your face? Then the **32nd** Annual Thanksgiving Turkey Trot 5k might be for you!!!

## Lace Up For The **32nd** Turkey Trot!

Running Together- **In Person**

Springfield is rockin out the Trot **in person this year, rain or shine, on Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, at 8 a.m. in downtown Springfield.**

**You can [register by clicking here](https://www.parkboard.org/TurkeyTrot) (online or in person), and the official site has the race route map so you can see where you will run (or if you don’t enjoy torture, walk) all of that 5K.**

**Then show up on Thanksgiving morning, ready to prove how amazing you are!**

### What to do Next

[img: turkey trot participants]

**There is a chance to see other weirdos… ehem, I mean running enthusiast, at packet pickup on Wednesday, November 25th, from 8 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. at the Springfield Expo Center, 635 E. St. Louis. Packet pickup is also available on race day from 6 to 7:15 a.m.**

If you haven’t already registered online (**[here](https://www.parkboard.org/TurkeyTrot)**) you will have a chance to register on site at this event.

Part of your Trot registration fee will go towards [Springfield-Greene County Park Board Youth Recreation Scholarship Fund](https://www.parkboard.org/439/Scholarships) and the [Developmental Center of the Ozarks](https://www.dcoonline.com/).

While you’re picking up your packet, you can also help our world a little more by bringing along **seven nonperishable food items** to donate to Ozarks Food Harvest who will be collecting donations there in one of their largest food drives of the year.

## Run, 417, Run

Whether you’re running with a big group, joining a few family members on a route, or hitting the pavement alone, you are part of a great event benefiting 417 land!

So lace up, go run off those Thanksgiving feast calories, and then give yourself a well deserved pat on the back.
