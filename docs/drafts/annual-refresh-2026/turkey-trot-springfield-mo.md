By Carla Broderick

Does running entice you? Do you get a thrill from pounding pavement in the painfully cold mornings of late fall? Does the thought of helping a good cause while getting a free t-shirt bring a smile to your face? Then the **32nd** Annual Thanksgiving Turkey Trot 5k might be for you!!! **[CHECK: free t-shirt perk comes from the 2020 post; not confirmed for 2026 in the verified row]**

## Lace Up For The **32nd** Turkey Trot!

Running Together- **In Person**

Springfield is rockin out the Trot **in person this year: Thursday, Nov. 26, 2026, at 8 a.m. in downtown Springfield.**

After you’re all registered up, you can **[register by clicking here](https://www.parkboard.org/TurkeyTrot)**, then choose from one of the five routes listed on the official site and run (or if you don’t enjoy torture, walk) all of that 5K sometime during Thanksgiving weekend. **[CHECK: this paragraph (five routes, run any time over Thanksgiving weekend) describes the 2020 virtual Trot and conflicts with the in-person 2026 race; rewrite from the official page]**

Next, just upload your run times and photos to **[ParkBoard.org/TurkeyTrot](https://www.parkboard.org/TurkeyTrot)** by midnight on Sunday, November 29th to prove how amazing you are and maybe even win some prizes! **[CHECK: 2020 virtual-submission deadline (midnight Sunday, Nov 29); delete or replace]**

### What to do Next

[img: turkey trot participants]

Even though there will not be a mass gathering for the actual run this year, there will be a chance to see other weirdos… ehem, I mean running enthusiast, at the packet pickup event on Wednesday, November 25th. **[CHECK: 'no mass gathering' is 2020 wording; Wed Nov 25 pickup matches the official page (checked Nov 13) but is not in the CSV row, so confirm hours before publishing]**

If you haven’t already registered online (**[here](https://www.parkboard.org/TurkeyTrot)**) you will have a chance to register on site at this event.

Part of your Trot registration fee will go towards [Springfield-Greene County Park Board Youth Recreation Scholarship Fund](https://www.parkboard.org/439/Scholarships) and the [Developmental Center of the Ozarks](https://www.dcoonline.com/).

While you’re picking up your packet, you can also help our world a little more by bringing along some food to donate to Ozarks Food Harvest who will be collecting donations there in one of their largest food drives of the year.

## Run, 417, Run

Whether you’re running with a big group, joining a few family members on a route, or hitting the pavement alone, you are part of a great event benefiting 417 land!

So lace up, go run off those Thanksgiving feast calories, and then give yourself a well deserved pat on the back.
