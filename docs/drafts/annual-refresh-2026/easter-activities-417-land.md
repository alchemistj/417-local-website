# Easter Activities in 417 Land

March 12, 2021

By Carla Broderick

**Update: this roundup was written for Easter 2021, so check each organizer’s site for this year’s dates and details.**

Hippity Hoppity Easter’s on its way!

There are some great events to get involved with this Spring! Here are some Easter activities and egg hunts going on around Springfield and the surrounding 417 land:

## Easter Egg-stravaganza

Ozark Community Center

**Check the organizer’s site for this year’s date**

The OC will be hosting an easter egg hunt for two different age groups so every child gets a chance to grab eggs at their event. Kids and adults alike can also have fun with yard games, crafts, face painting, and more! Hop on over to the OC Pavillion and start your Easter fun!


## Easter Day At The Farm

Little Beau’s Backyard

**Check the organizer’s site for this year’s date**

Who can resist the chance to visit with some baby bunnies and chicks?!?

Come see them at Little Beau’s Backyard and visit other furry friends while you’re there.

Once you’re done hanging out with the adorable animals, you can continue the fun with some easter egg decorating!

Get all the details [here](https://www.littlebeausbackyard.com/)

## Easter Egg Hunt – in the POOL!

Pat Jones YMCA

**Check the organizer’s site for this year’s date**

YMCA is filling their pool full of eggs! You’re invited to come dive on in and grab those eggs as they float around with you. So fun!

## Easter Cookie Bouquet Class taught by Julie’s Cookie Jar

Rustic Romance Vintage Marketplace

**Check the organizer’s site for this year’s date**

Have you ever seen a beautiful Easter cookie and wished you knew how to create something like that? Well now’s your chance!

The cookie decorating class will be two hours filled with easy how-to instructions, hands on experience, and all the tools (and sugary yummines) you need to make your easter cookie masterpiece.

Get tickets [here](https://checkout.square.site/buy/VQPZ3ZZHTV7SRH47UCMJY3DB).

## Easter Day Fun!

Thompson Flower Farm

**Check the organizer’s site for this year’s date**

There will be all sorts of Easter day fun to be had at Thompson Flower Farm!

You’ll be surrounded with music, vendors, food, games, and of course, an Easter egg hunt!

Get all the details [here](https://www.facebook.com/events/122920826413764/?acontext=%7B%22event_action_history%22%3A[%7B%22mechanism%22%3A%22calendar_interested_events_unit%22%2C%22surface%22%3A%22bookmark_calendar%22%7D]%7D).

## Easter Egg Hunt

Nixa Parks and Recreation

**Check the organizer’s site for this year’s date**

Nixa is the place to be if you want to spot the Easter Bunny (and maybe even snap a picture with him).

There will also be multiple easter egg hunts divided by age group so every kiddo has a fair chance to grab those eggs.


## Easter Family Fun Day

Renovation

**Check the organizer’s site for this year’s date**

Everyone is invited to come enjoy some Easter fun at Renovation Church!

This is a free event that will include crafts, a petting zoo, bucket train rides, an Easter egg hunt, and more.

All the info is [here](https://www.facebook.com/events/120256440017355/?acontext=%7B%22event_action_history%22%3A[%7B%22mechanism%22%3A%22calendar_interested_events_unit%22%2C%22surface%22%3A%22bookmark_calendar%22%7D]%7D).

## Storytime: “How To Catch the Easter Bunny”

Art Zone

**Check the organizer’s site for this year’s date**

The Art Zone is holding an Easter themed story time!

They’ll be reading “How to Catch the Easter Bunny” and glazing a ceramic.


## All Day Indoor Egg Hunt

Discovery Center Springfield

**Check the organizer’s site for this year’s date**

Easter eggs will be hidden throughout the Discovery Center for the ultimate indoor hunt.

Peter Rabbit will also be making an appearance!

The Easter activities are free with the general admission.

All the details are [here](https://www.discoverycenter.org/easter/).
