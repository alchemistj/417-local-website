One of the best feelings in the world is growing your own food. If you need help getting a garden started, Arlie's Farm in Rogersville has hosted free Gardening 101 workshops to teach you how to grow your own food with confidence.

## The Where

Arlie's Farm is a charming farm about 6 miles outside of Springfield, MO. It has what you'd expect from a farm: growing vegetables, a barnyard, farm animals and plenty of scenery.

They've also added some unexpected extras, including stroll gardens, a greenhouse and a bed and breakfast called [The Bombay Getaway](https://www.vrbo.com/1486970), so you can stay overnight and enjoy the quiet. If you're planning a romantic trip, see our list of [date night ideas in Springfield](/best-date-night-ideas-in-springfield-mo/).

## The What

The [Gardening 101 class](https://www.facebook.com/Arliesfarm/) is designed to give you the information you need to get vegetables growing. Their experienced gardener teaches composting, propagating and plenty of tips and tricks to turn your thumb green.

The farm has suggested that you wear your farming clothes and bring a notebook and a sack lunch.

## The When

Workshops have been held on spring weekends, typically from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. Dates change from year to year, so check the farm's [Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/Arliesfarm/) for the current schedule.

## Go Get Your Gardening On

There are many benefits to learning how to grow your own fruits and vegetables. Getting tips from people who have spent decades playing in the dirt is a good place to start.

Workshop seats are limited, so reserve your spot early. Reservation details are on the farm's Facebook page.
