Summer is long, and the right camp can fill it with memories. Springfield and the surrounding 417 area have camps for your athlete, artist, dancer, tech whiz, actor, animal lover, scientist, crafter and more.

Camp dates, ages and prices change every year, so see each camp's website for 2026 pricing and current sessions. Many camps open registration in winter and fill up early.

## AR Workshop

AR Workshop is a hands-on craft studio. Its youth summer art camps have kids making projects such as tie-dye, canvas pillows, string art, yarn wreaths and plank trays.

The studio is at [1550 E. Battlefield in Springfield](https://www.google.com/maps/place/AR+Workshop+Springfield/@37.1587959,-93.2689638,17z). See the [summer camp options](https://www.arworkshop.com/summer-camps/) and the [Springfield schedule](https://www.arworkshop.com/springfield/), or call [(417) 429-0803](tel:+14174290803).

Cost: see website for 2026 pricing.

## Code Ninjas

Code Ninjas teaches kids coding through games. Past camp themes include programming video games, making stop-motion movies and building in Roblox.

The Springfield center is [near Republic Road and Kansas Expressway](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Code+Ninjas/@37.1379318,-93.3447331,15z). Call [(417) 319-7996](tel:+14173197996) to ask about this summer's camps.

Cost: see website for 2026 pricing.

## Cub Creek Science Camp

[Cub Creek Science Camp](https://cubcreeksciencecamp.com/) is a traditional overnight camp with a science and animal focus. Campers ages 7 to 17 can pick from activities such as rope courses and survival skills, alongside time with animals.

Cost: see website for 2026 pricing.

## Dance Mission

Dance Mission is [near Fremont and Republic Road in Springfield](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Dance+Mission/@37.1352345,-93.270858,17z). Along with dance, acrobatics and aerial work, the studio emphasizes character, confidence and giving back.

Its [short summer camps](https://www.dancemission.org/summer-camps) have included themed options in dance and painting, dance and plants, and circus skills, each ending with a performance for family and friends. Call [(417) 894-1187](tel:+14178941187) with questions.

Cost: see website for 2026 pricing.

## Day Camps and More at the War Zone

Springfield has a [full-size Nerf arena](https://www.thewarzonespringfield.com/). The War Zone's camps and events have included Jedi training and Nerf coaching, with a focus on building life skills in local kids.

Check the [War Zone's Facebook events page](https://www.facebook.com/Warzonespringfieldmo/events/) for upcoming camps.

## Dickerson Park Zoo

Dickerson Park Zoo runs hands-on classes for kids from toddlers to teenagers rather than traditional camps. Kids get close to animals and learn what it takes to care for them.

See the zoo's [education calendar](https://dickersonparkzoo.org/education/education-calendar/) for current classes and prices.

## Discovery Center

The Discovery Center of Springfield offers week-long camps for young scientists and adventurers. Past themes have covered solving crimes, hunting dinosaurs, space, oceans and wizardry.

Call [(417) 862-9910](tel:+14178629910) or visit the [Discovery Center's site](https://www.discoverycenter.org/) for current camps.

Cost: see website for 2026 pricing, including member rates.

## Discovery Garden Montessori School

[Discovery Garden's summer camps](https://www.dgmontessori.org/programs/summer-camps) follow Montessori principles and are built around hands-on weekly themes such as weather, the Olympics, music and the universe. The school has served children from 16 months to 10 years old.

The school is at [1515 S. National Ave.](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Discovery+Garden+Montessori+School/@37.186142,-93.27744,19z), inside National Avenue Christian Church. Call [(417) 631-4590](tel:+14176314590) for details.

Cost: see website for 2026 pricing.

## The OC

The Ozark Community Center has run full-summer camps for school-age kids, with field trips, swimming, crafts and games. Older campers have had activities such as ziplining and canoeing.

Call [(417) 581-7002](tel:+14175817002) for this year's camp information, ages and rates.

Cost: see website for 2026 pricing, including member rates.

## Ozark Dance Academy

Ozark Dance Academy has offered short, themed dance camps for kids ages 3 to 10, with themes drawn from favorite movies and stories. Call the studio to confirm its current location and camp dates before you register.

Call [(417) 581-7101](tel:+14175817101) for current camps.

Cost: see website for 2026 pricing.

## Southern Missouri Arts Connection

The "Something for Everyone" art camp from SMAC has offered multiple options for ages 6 to 17, from stained glass and sculpture to comics and pop art. Camps run in June and July and have mostly been held at the Southern Missouri Arts Connection in Hollister.

See [SMAC's camp listing](https://www.springfieldarts.org/node/3203) or call (417) 598-1568 for details.

Cost: see website for 2026 pricing.

## SPARC

The Springfield-Greene County Park Board has a camp for just about every interest. Programs have covered outdoor adventures like cycling and kayaking, plus hockey, figure skating, golf, equestrian and zoo programs.

See the [Park Board website](https://www.parkboard.org/) for current camp listings.

Cost: varies by program.

## Springfield Ballet

Springfield Ballet is in the Creamery Arts Center at [411 N. Sherman Pkwy](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Springfield+Ballet/@37.211653,-93.281824,17z). Its [summer dance programs](https://springfieldballet.org/Summer-Programs) have served kids ages 3 to 14.

Younger dancers get themed classes with dancing, crafts, story time and a snack. Older dancers can take camps that add nutrition, dance history and musical theatre. Call [417-862-1343](tel:+14178621343) for details.

Cost: see website for 2026 pricing.

## Springfield Little Theatre

Springfield Little Theatre has offered summer programs from one-day workshops to multi-week camps, for preschoolers through high schoolers, from beginner to advanced. Topics include acting, dance, tech and more.

Find the current camps on the [theatre's website](https://www.springfieldlittletheatre.org/) or call [(417) 869-1334](tel:+14178691334).

Cost: varies, and sibling discounts have been available.

## Springfield Warrior Sports

Springfield Warrior Sports runs ninja-style [summer camps](https://www.springfieldwarriorsports.com/summer-camps-in-springfield-mo/) where kids build strength and agility on obstacles like those on American Ninja Warrior. Call [417-894-7319](tel:+14178947319) to register or ask questions.

Cost: see website for 2026 pricing.

## The Summit Camp Raven

Summit Preparatory School hosts [themed summer camps](https://www.thesummitprep.org/summer/) in art, music, STEM and more, for pre-K through middle school grade levels. Sessions have ranged from a single day to a full week.

See the school [on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Summit+Preparatory+School/@37.1520637,-93.292992,17z) or call [(417) 869-8077](tel:+14178698077) for more information.

Cost: see website for 2026 pricing.

## Wonders of Wildlife

[Wonders of Wildlife's week-long camps](https://wondersofwildlife.org/education/camps/) cover biology, chemistry, earth science, engineering, fishing and survival skills, led by the museum's educators. Scholarships have been available.

Cost: see website for 2026 pricing.

## YMCA

The YMCA of the Ozarks runs themed week-long day camps at locations in Springfield. Kids get plenty of physical activity and the chance to meet new friends.

See the [YMCA's Springfield summer day camp page](https://www.orymca.org/) for locations, themes and member and non-member rates.

## YMCA Camp Wakonda

[Camp Wakonda](https://www.campwakonda.org/) is in Ash Grove, Missouri, and offers many week-long sessions for kids from 3rd grade through high school. Each session has its own theme, with a focus on the outdoors, friendships and building confidence.

Cost: see website for 2026 pricing. Financial assistance has been available.

## Choosing a Camp

A few questions help narrow the list:

- What does your child enjoy most: art, animals, movement, building or being outside?
- Do you need a half-day, full-day or overnight option?
- Does the camp offer sibling or member discounts?
- When does registration open? Popular sessions fill early.
