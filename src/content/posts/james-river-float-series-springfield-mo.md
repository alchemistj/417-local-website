One of the best ways to explore 417 is floating the James River.

This 130-mile river was a key factor in the early exploration and settlement of the Ozarks. It forms Lake Springfield and provides drinking water for the city of Springfield.

But most importantly, it is super fun to float on. Okay, maybe not most importantly. Supplying water to a whole city is pretty important. But the floating is still high on the list of its value.

That is why the James River Basin Partnership (JRBP) teamed up with 37 North Expeditions to create the James River Float Series. Here is how it worked.

## The When

The float series ran in 2021 and 2022, one Saturday a month, with each trip covering a different stretch of the river and the final trip of the 2021 series an overnight float.

For current trips, see [37northexpeditions.com](https://www.37northexpeditions.com/) and the James River Basin Partnership.

## The What

Each float trip was meant to be fun on the river and a chance to meet new people, with some education about floating the James River.

Depending on the trip, there was a focus on topics like floating etiquette, safely navigating rivers, creatures living in the James, the history of the river and more. Fun and educational.

## The Where

Each trip started with floaters meeting at Springfield Brewing Company. From there, 37 North Expeditions shuttled the group to that day's starting point on the river.

Floating every trip in the series covered an impressive stretch of the James River. After the float, the outfitter shuttled everyone back to their cars.

## Get Out There

There is a lot to explore in 417 land, and the rivers are part of what makes the area so appealing. Floating the James is a great way to see one.

Browse [trips from 37 North Expeditions](https://www.37northexpeditions.com/all-adventures) and check with the James River Basin Partnership for what is on now. See you on the river.
