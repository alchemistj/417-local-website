Here are free blog posts, guides and tools for building a successful online presence, with the key elements you need to grow your reach and bring in consistent new customers.

Already read it? Let's dive in, beginning with the most important strategy for a local business: reviews.

## Start With Reviews

Most customers read reviews before they visit a local business, and many will only consider businesses with a strong star rating. To stand out, do everything in your control to earn and manage your online reviews.

How? Learn how to automate the acquisition of [more small business reviews in 7 steps](/get-more-small-business-reviews/).

## Set Up Your Google Business Profile

Once you're consistently improving your reviews, it makes sense to start marketing to reach new customers. Fresh, recent reviews make you look great when new people find you.

Since most small businesses have a service address or a defined service area, it's important to dial in your Google Business Profile first. Once that's done, you can ignore all of those "Google" calls telling you you aren't verified.

Learn how to [create and optimize your free Google Business Profile](/create-google-my-business-listing/).

## Build Consistent Citations

Once your address is verified with Google, you have what's called a NAP defined:

- NAP = Business Name, Address and Phone Number.

The next step is to repeat that NAP on other sites that list businesses for potential customers. Those listings are called citations.

You can see most of the important citations to acquire in [this BrightLocal guide](https://www.brightlocal.com/resources/top-citation-sites/industry/). You can also learn about the [SEO companies in the Springfield area that perform citation services](/best-citation-services-in-springfield/).

Once your business info is consistent across citation sites, add your NAP to your own website too.

If you don't have a website yet, learn how to build an [affordable website for your small business](/building-small-business-websites/).

With your NAP on your website and your citations, you create a web of consistent information for Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo and other search engines. That helps your rankings, because search engines can tell who you are, where you are and what you do.

## Get Traffic From Google

After you have a website, citations and a system for getting reviews (or a [review management service](/get-more-small-business-reviews/)), the next step is traffic.

You can get free traffic from Google to your website. Optimizing for that traffic is called SEO, which stands for search engine optimization.

The foundation of SEO is making sure Google knows your website exists. This guide shows you how to [connect Google Analytics and Google Search Console to your website](/how-to-connect-site-to-google/), which is the first step.

Then [the best SEO companies in Springfield](/best-seo-in-springfield-mo/) can help you find and rank for the keywords that matter to your business, the phrases people type into Google, to bring in traffic that generates leads and sales.

Depending on your type of business, you can also bring traffic to your site with social media posts.
