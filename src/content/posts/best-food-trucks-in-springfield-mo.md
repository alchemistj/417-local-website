If you're down to try some food truck goodness in the #SGF area but aren't sure which direction to head, start here. These are some of our favorite food trucks in Springfield, including pizza, Hawaiian food, street tacos, British pasties and free ice cream.

We aim to document the town, but we can't find everything yet. If your favorite food truck (or your own truck) isn't listed, [reach out](/contact-us/) and we'll add it.

Trucks move, change menus and change hours, so check each truck's social page before you go.

## Speedy's Cowabunga

[Speedy's Cowabunga](https://www.facebook.com/speedyscowabunga/) is a stationary food truck serving authentic Hawaiian and Island-style food in the Springfield area.

**What they offer:** Wings, shrimp, Luau Chicken Tacos, Kalua Pork Tacos and pulled-pork sliders, plus fries and mini egg rolls. If you want an Island food fix in Springfield, this is the place.

**Must try:** The Speedy's Wing Meal: six wings basted in your flavor of choice, served with signature fries and honey biscuits.

**Where to find them:** The truck has been set up at 441 S Kimbrough Ave in Springfield. Check their Facebook page to confirm before you head out.

## The Pitch

The Pitch Pizza & Pub's food truck is a condensed version of what they serve at their restaurant on E. Sunshine: pizza and calzones, with a range of classic and not-so-classic options.

**Must try:** The classic Pitch Pizza, with pesto, pepperoni, salami, pepperoncinis, red onions, fresh mozzarella and a balsamic reduction drizzle. It's a fresh take on an old Italian standard.

**Where to find them:** The truck has been parked by 4 by 4 Brewing Co. in the heart of [Galloway Village](/galloway-village-springfield-mo/), at 2811 E Galloway St. Check their Facebook or Instagram for where they'll be this week.

## Cold Patrol

The Cold Patrol is a free ice cream truck run by Springfield police officers. Hiland Dairy donates ice cream each year for officers to "serve" to the community.

**What they offer:** Free ice cream, plus smiles from local officers. The goal is to help families see their police officers as approachable people who are there to help whenever needed.

**Must try:** Any flavor you can get, but especially a thank-you to the officers who serve Springfield every day.

**Where to find them:** There's no single location for the Cold Patrol, and the truck turns up all over town. Keep an eye out, and check Facebook or Instagram for where they'll be this week.

## Pineapple Whip

![Kids enjoying pineapple whip](./images/best-food-trucks-in-springfield-mo/kids-enjoying-pineapple-whip.jpg)

Pineapple Whip is a remarkable "ice cream" dessert that's like no other.

**What they offer:** Lots of flavors to try, including the original pineapple (the same recipe since 1974), strawberry-kiwi, banana-pomegranate and more. They also have had a punch card for earning free whips. Check their site for current prices.

**Must try:** Start with the original pineapple whip. What else has been going strong for more than 50 years?

**Where to find them:** Pineapple Whip has four locations in Springfield. Search for the one nearest you on Google Maps.

## Tacos El Champu

Stop at this truck for authentic Mexican food that is made fresh and full of flavor.

**What they offer:** [Tacos El Champu](https://www.facebook.com/tacoselchampu/) serves just about anything Mexican that fits in a shell. There are several street taco options (I can't decide whether I love the shrimp or the asada more), burritos as big as your face, crispy quesadillas and plenty of other choices. Don't forget to grab some of their salsa with anything you order.

**Prices:** Street tacos have been among the most affordable items on the menu. Check their page for current prices.

**Must try:** You could try almost anything here and your taste buds would be doing the happy dance. I'd suggest trying four different tacos to start. They're made fresh and topped with cilantro and onions, among other things, depending on the taco.

**Where to find them:** Tacos El Champu, alongside a few other food trucks, can be found at the front of the parking lot at [2348 S Campbell Ave in Springfield](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Tacos+El+Champu/@37.172003,-93.2955734,19z/data=!4m13!1m7!3m6!1s0x87cf6379318ad80d:0xac0ec503c752304d!2s2348+S+Campbell+Ave,+Springfield,+MO+65807!3b1!8m2!3d37.1716311!4d-93.2942564!3m4!1s0x87cf6360afccf509:0xae659544e60b461e!8m2!3d37.1721947!4d-93.295134). Look for the red truck in the middle.

## London Calling Pasty Company

[London Calling Pasty Company](https://www.facebook.com/LondonCallingPastyCompany/) is a British food truck that specializes in gourmet pasties and classic British dishes. If you're not familiar, a pasty is a buttery, flaky pie crust shell stuffed with savory ingredients. You can get steak, onion, potato, rutabaga and more inside a pasty here.

**What they offer:** Traditional (and not-so-traditional) pasties, plus classic British dishes like shepherd's pie and chips (fries). London Calling seems committed to bringing the taste of London to the streets of Springfield, and it succeeds in true British style. They've also run meal deals from time to time, so ask about specials.

**Must try:** Go traditional with the "Oggie," a pasty stuffed with tender steak, golden potato, onion and rutabaga. Don't skip the "Bangers & Mash" either: sausage seasoned with herbs and parmesan, buried in whipped mashed potatoes and stuffed into a flaky crust.

**Where to find them:** Check their Facebook page for current locations and hours.

## Tips for Finding Food Trucks in Springfield

Food trucks often post their location and hours for the day on Facebook or Instagram, so follow your favorites. Many park at breweries, shopping center lots and community events. [Galloway Village](/galloway-village-springfield-mo/) and the local breweries are good places to look, and a truck at a brewery makes dinner easy.
