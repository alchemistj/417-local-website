Café Cusco has been serving Peruvian-inspired food in Springfield since 2013, and it does it right.

With options that include gluten free and vegan, Café Cusco has something for everyone.

This hidden gem is at [234 E Commercial St](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Caf%C3%A9+Cusco/@37.2294218,-93.2929476,17z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m5!3m4!1s0x87cf62f2d97886ff:0x718c2d3e6e945f33!8m2!3d37.2294218!4d-93.2907589) in Springfield, in a restored building constructed in 1883.

## My choice

Everything I have tried at Café Cusco has been fantastic, so it is hard to choose just one dish. My main pick, and the one I suggest you order, is the Chicken Aji Verde.

Move over, Moroccan chicken. The Chicken Aji Verde is the chicken dish to beat among exotic chicken dishes.

### The deets on the dish

If you are not familiar with Peruvian food (I was not, before I discovered Café Cusco), aji verde is a traditional Peruvian green sauce. Once you try it, you will want to pour it on everything.

Café Cusco pours the sauce over a blend of peppers, onions, tomatoes and chicken sautéed in white wine. There is a side of rice to help you scoop up every last drop of that aji verde.

## What are you waiting for?

There is an abundance of happiness to be found in the cuisine 417 land has to offer.

Start exploring by heading to Café Cusco and treating yourself to an order of the Chicken Aji Verde.
