Wingapalooza 2026 is Saturday, November 7 at the Springfield Expo Center. Doors run from 11 a.m. to 5 p.m., and your ticket gets you wing samples from local restaurants, all in one building.

## Wingapalooza 2026 at a Glance

- **Date:** Saturday, November 7, 2026
- **Time:** 11 a.m. to 5 p.m.
- **Where:** Springfield Expo Center, 635 E. St. Louis St., Springfield, MO 65806
- **Put on by:** Sertoma Clubs of Springfield
- **Tickets:** $25 online in advance, $30 at the door ([wingapalooza.com](https://wingapalooza.com))

## What to Expect

Wingapalooza is a wing tasting event. Restaurants and businesses from around the Springfield area set up booths and hand out samples, and they compete for the event's awards, including a Facebook Choice Award voted on by the public. Go hungry.

Expect a crowd, and plan on lines at the popular booths. There is live music and a wing eating contest during the day. The schedule is on the official site.

The night before, on Friday, November 6, Sertoma also hosts a Texas Hold 'Em poker tournament with live music at the Expo Center. Details are on the official site.

## Tickets

Advance tickets are $25 online through [wingapalooza.com](https://wingapalooza.com), or $30 at the door on the day. You can also buy them at Hy-Vee and Rapid Robert's stores.

## Tips for Going

- Buy ahead. It saves $5 and you skip the ticket line.
- Eat light beforehand and wear something you do not mind getting sauce on.
- Bring wet wipes. Napkins run out.
- Get there early if you want the shortest lines.

## Where the Money Goes

Sertoma runs Wingapalooza as a fundraiser. The Springfield Sertoma Clubs have supported the Boys & Girls Clubs of Springfield since 1941 and focus on at-risk youth in southwest Missouri. So the wings are for a good cause.

## A Bit of History

Wingapalooza is a long-running Springfield tradition. In 2020, Sertoma ran it as the Wingapalooza Crawl instead of one big event. That version used a booklet of vouchers you could redeem at participating restaurants through the end of the year.

The big gathering is back, but details can change. Always confirm times, prices and rules on the [official Wingapalooza site](https://wingapalooza.com) before you go. After November 7, check wingapalooza.com for the date of the next event.
