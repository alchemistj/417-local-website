Everyone is a little Irish on St. Patrick's Day, right?

Whether you are a wee lad or an old lass, you will find some Celtic fun in and around Springfield, MO.

**Note:** The events below have run in past years, and most are annual. Dates and details change every year, so use each link to check what is planned. They are listed roughly in the order they tend to fall around March 17.

Let's dive in.

## St. Patrick's clay creating

**Art Zone**

Hone your Irish pride into an artful creation. Art Zone in Nixa has offered St. Patrick's clay projects such as pot-o-gold pieces, leprechaun hat vases and shamrock photo holders. Past sessions have been open to all ages, with the option to stay at the studio or take a kit to go.

See [Art Zone](https://www.artzonenixa.com/) for the current events calendar.

## Conservatory of the Ozarks St. Patrick's recital

**The Creamery Arts Center**

Looking for a more relaxing way to celebrate? The Conservatory of the Ozarks has held a recital of Celtic and Irish-inspired music for St. Patrick's Day at the Creamery Arts Center. Contact the Conservatory of the Ozarks for this year's date and details.

## Art camp: Luck of the Irish

**Art Zone**

Art Zone has also offered an Irish-themed art camp for kids, with painting in the morning and sculpture work after lunch. Past camps welcomed kids ages 5 and older.

Check [Art Zone's site](https://www.artzonenixa.com/) for camp dates and pricing.

## Shamrocks and Shenanigans

**Fleet Feet Springfield**

Fleet Feet Springfield and Brooks Running have hosted a St. Patrick's Day race day with distances including a 15K, a 5K and a beer mile, followed by live music and an after party with food, beer and more.

Get the current details on [RunSignup](https://runsignup.com/Race/MO/Springfield/ShamrocksandShenanigansFleetFeetSpringfield).

## St. Patrick's Day pub crawl

**Downtown Springfield**

Get out and celebrate the way plenty of people do, with a pub crawl. A downtown St. Patrick's Day crawl has run in past years, with a complimentary first drink at the starting pub, drink specials at participating pubs and an after party at the final stop. Costume contests have been part of the fun, so wear your best green.

Search for the current year's St. Patrick's Day crawl in downtown Springfield to find ticket information.

## St. Patty's at Springfield Brewing Company

**Springfield Brewing Company**

Springfield Brewing Company has celebrated St. Patrick's Day with food and beer specials, and it has made a week of it with live music at times. Follow the [Springfield Brewing Company Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/Springfieldbrewingco) for details.

## Dublin's Pass Downtown

**Dublin's Pass**

One of the best Irish pubs in town is naturally a St. Patrick's Day destination. Dublin's Pass has served traditional Irish dishes like corned beef and cabbage, poured green beer and brought in live music for the day.

See the [Dublin's Pass website](https://www.dublinspass.com/home) for what they have planned.

## Finnegan's Wake

**Finnegan's Wake**

With a name like Finnegan's Wake, you know they know how to throw an Irish party. Past celebrations have included a Drunken Bruncheon, a spot in the downtown St. Patrick's pub crawl and an all-day party on St. Patrick's Day with live music, green beer and Irish whiskey.

Keep up with their plans on [Finnegan's Wake's Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/finneganssgf/).

## And one extra: St. Patrick's Day parade in Eureka Springs

**Eureka Springs, Arkansas**

Springfield's own St. Patrick's Day parade has not always been held, but Eureka Springs, Arkansas, is not too far a drive and has parade festivities of its own, followed by plenty of time to hang out in town with live music, great food and some blarney.

Check with Eureka Springs for this year's parade date, route and times before you make the drive.
