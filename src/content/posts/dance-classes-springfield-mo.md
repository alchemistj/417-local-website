Looking for a fun thing to do in 417?

These local dance classes in Springfield make it easy to create a unique experience, by yourself or as a couple.

Many studios also offer packages for "First Dance" training, dance clubs and season-long events if you want something more than a one-off.

Whether your goal is to make a memory, have fun or get fit, here are a few of the top locally owned dance studios that offer adult dance classes in Springfield.

## Ballroom World

Ballroom World, at [1435 E. Independence St., Ste. 105](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Ballroom+World+LLC/@37.1445931,-93.270343,17z) in Springfield, is a fun way to get into ballroom dance, wedding dance choreography and more, with individual and couples group lessons.

It may be the most romantic option for couples, and a group lesson makes an easy date night in Springfield. See the website for current lesson pricing.

In addition to single lessons, they offer a long list of packages for weddings or for anyone who wants to commit to a season of fun. The packages include private and group lessons and dance parties.

Check their current calendar of events and pricing on [their website](https://www.theballroomworld.com/).

## Dance With Me

Have fun, get fit and make friends at Dance With Me on Commercial Street in Springfield.

You can learn a variety of social dances, including country, ballroom and salsa. You can also join the [Springfield Swing Dancing Club](https://www.dancewithme.us/swing-springfield/), which meets with the local pros at Dance With Me.

They offer online classes and unique events too, such as learning the choreography to Michael Jackson's "Thriller" in their [C-Street Zombie Corps classes](https://www.dancewithme.us/c-street-zombie-corps/).

You don't need a partner, though bringing one is welcome. Dance With Me is at [224 E. Commercial St., Springfield, MO 65803](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Dance+With+Me/@37.2294489,-93.293211,17z).

See all their current offerings on [their website](https://www.dancewithme.us/).

## Step by Step Dance Studio

With group classes and one-on-one lessons, Step by Step in Springfield will take you from stumbling to looking like a pro.

They teach ballroom, east and west coast swing, salsa, line dancing and more, so there are many options for a night out or a whole season.

They also have lessons and packages to help you feel confident in, or even choreograph, your first dance on the big day.

See [Step by Step's current offerings](https://dancestepbystep.com/). The studio is behind FedEx on Glenstone, at 1722 S. Glenstone, Suite G, Springfield, MO 65804.

## Picking a Dance Class

A few quick questions can narrow the choice:

- Do you want social dancing, wedding choreography or a fitness workout?
- Are you coming alone or with a partner? Dance With Me says no partner is required.
- Would you rather try a single lesson first or sign up for a package?
