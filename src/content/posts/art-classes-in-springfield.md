Ready to get creative? Whether you want in-person or online, adult or kids' classes, a full 8 to 10 week course or a one-time experience, there is a creative outlet in Springfield for you.

The options range from painting, woodworking and glassblowing to stress-reducing knitting and more. Here's a list of art classes in Springfield.

Prices listed are from 2021 unless noted. Class schedules and prices change, so check each studio's site, and call to confirm where noted.

## AR Workshop

Bring your favorite drinks and make trendy DIY home decor with step-by-step instruction. It's a fun fit for group events, parties, showers or hangouts. All materials and tools are provided.

AR Workshop is in the Galleria Shopping Center across from Hobby Lobby, at [1550 E. Battlefield Rd. Ste M-2](https://www.google.com/maps/place/AR+Workshop+Springfield/@37.1587959,-93.2689638,17z).

Cost: projects started at $15 in 2021. Check their site for current pricing.

Call [(417) 429-0803](tel:+14174290803) or see [their Springfield page](https://www.arworkshop.com/springfield/) for all they offer.

## Arlie's Farm Painting Class

Bring your friends and craft with local artist Katie Hopkins, who has led new art projects at this creative adult happy hour. Some sessions are painting, others are crafting, and you have free rein over supplies. Bring your favorite drink and maybe extras to share.

The class is just a few minutes east of Springfield, [past Turners](https://www.google.com/maps/place/2346+S+Farm+Rd+237,+Rogersville,+MO+65742/@37.167437,-93.1094727,17z).

Call [(417) 929-3276](tel:+14179293276) to confirm current dates and pricing before you go.

## Beautiful Fight Woodworking

Create a DIY wood project with Beautiful Fight Woodworking. You get step-by-step guidance, and all the materials and tools are provided. It's a fun way to make something you'll keep for years.

Cost: about $30 on average in 2021.

Beautiful Fight is on historic C-Street, just east of Grant, at [301 E. Commercial St.](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/37.1105177,-93.0580894/beautiful+fight+woodworking+springfield+mo/@37.1700635,-93.3143694,11z). Call [(417) 848-9327](tel:+14178489327) to confirm they're taking classes and to ask about upcoming dates.

## Creava Studio

Creava Studio offers classes for exploring your artistic side in a warm, welcoming environment. Past classes have included pottery, drawing, painting and clay modeling, with something for everyone in the family.

Cost: classes started at $60 in 2021.

Creava Studio is at [1637 E. St. Louis St.](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Creava+Studio/@37.2095349,-93.2673237,17z), a little west of Glenstone Ave. Call [(417) 880-3531](tel:+14178803531) to check what's currently on offer.

## Firehouse Pottery

Firehouse Pottery is the spot for pottery painting. They serve drinks, including adult beverages, and it works for parties and group events, drop-in solo creating or a relaxing night at home with a pottery-to-go package.

Cost: about $10 to $12 per painter in 2021.

Find Firehouse Pottery at [4150 S. Lone Pine Ave.](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Firehouse+Pottery/@37.137018,-93.237542,17z) in Galloway Village. Visit [their site](https://www.firehousepottery.com/) or call [(417) 881-8381](tel:+14178818381).

## Meike Aton Workshops

Meike Aton is a well-known Springfield artist whose paintings hang in many places around 417 land. She has offered workshops throughout the year where you can learn her techniques.

Cost: workshops started at $95 in 2021.

Meike Aton Art is in the Woodhurst Office Park at [1200 E. Woodhurst Dr.](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Meike+Aton+Art/@37.1564317,-93.2766905,17z), just south of Montclair St. and east of S. National Ave. See her [workshops page](https://meikeaton.com/workshops/) for upcoming dates, and call to confirm before you sign up.

## Painting With a Twist

Painting with a Twist is a fun night out with friends. Bring your own wine (BYOW) and snacks while you learn to paint. No artistic skills are needed.

The studio is in the Shamrock Center at [3444 S. Campbell Ave., Suite G](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Painting+with+a+Twist/@37.151636,-93.295328,17z), just south of Walnut Lawn St.

Cost: classes started around $30 in 2021. Check [their website](https://www.paintingwithatwist.com/studio/springfield) for current classes and pricing, or call (417) 755-7760.

## Pellegrino's

Pellegrino's School of Music and Art has offered ongoing art classes in drawing, painting, mixed media and more, with private and group options.

Cost: about $67 per month in 2021.

Call (417) 887-2800 to confirm current art classes and pricing.

## RSVPaint

Plan a night out and create something alongside friends at RSVPaint. Order from the full-service bar and paint on glass, wood, canvas and more, or book a party or group event.

Cost: $25 to $38 per painter in 2021.

RSVPaint is downtown at [440 S. Campbell Ave.](https://www.google.com/maps/place/RSVPaint+LLC/@37.2059864,-93.2940781,18z), a block south of Walnut St. Call (417) 413-4331 to confirm event schedules.

## Splatter Art Studio

Splatter Art Studio is a not-for-profit space where artists come together to teach, so you'll find a variety of instructors and classes.

Cost: varies by class.

The studio is in Galloway Village at [4160 S. Lone Pine Ave.](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Splatter+Art+Studio/@37.136677,-93.237594,17z) Call (417) 890-6464 for current offerings.

## Springfield Pottery

Learn the fundamentals of working with clay in an eight-week class, or attend one of the special workshops offered during the year. Classes are open to anyone age 16 and over, with tools and materials provided and optional drop-in times for current students.

Springfield Pottery is just south of the intersection of Walnut and Campbell at [416 S. Campbell Ave. #2010](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Springfield+Pottery/@37.2065306,-93.2940611,17z).

Cost: $225 for an 8-week class in 2021. See their [class page](https://www.springfieldpottery.com/classes) for current pricing, or call (417) 864-4677.

## Springfield Art Museum

The Springfield Art Museum offers art classes for kids, teens and adults. While its main building on Brookside Drive is closed for renovation, the museum has been running classes at a temporary downtown location, the Art Study Club in the Wilhoit Building at 431 S. Jefferson.

See the museum's [class and workshop listings](https://www.sgfmuseum.org/161/Adult-Studio-Classes) for current offerings, or call (417) 837-5700.

## The Village Yarnery

The Village Yarnery is family-owned and run, with an inviting atmosphere like stepping into a hospitable home. It's a good place to expand your skills and learn something new. Join a social stitching time, or contact them about upcoming workshops at (417) 882-1662.

The shop is in the Village Shopping Center, directly behind Smith's restaurant, at [2776 S. Campbell Ave. #C](https://www.google.com/maps/place/The+Village+Yarnery/@37.1632334,-93.2943248,17z).
