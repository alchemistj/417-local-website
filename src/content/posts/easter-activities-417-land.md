Hippity hoppity, Easter is on its way.

Springfield and the surrounding 417 land have a good mix of egg hunts and spring events for families. The places below have hosted Easter activities in past years. Dates, times and details change every year, so check each venue's link or social page for what is planned this spring.

## Ozark Community Center egg hunt

**Ozark Community Center**

The Ozark Community Center has hosted an Easter egg hunt with separate age groups so every child gets a chance to grab eggs. Past events have included yard games, crafts, face painting and more at the community center pavilion.

Check with the [City of Ozark](https://www.ozarkmissouri.com) for this year's event calendar.

## Easter day at the farm

**Little Beau's Backyard**

Who can resist a chance to visit baby bunnies and chicks? Little Beau's Backyard has hosted an Easter day where families can see the animals, visit other farm friends and decorate Easter eggs.

Check Little Beau's Backyard for this year's date and details.

## Egg hunt in the pool

**Pat Jones YMCA**

The YMCA has filled its pool with eggs, and kids dive in to grab the eggs as they float around. Check the [Ozarks Regional YMCA site](https://www.orymca.org) for this year's schedule.

![A hand-lettered Happy Easter card with purple eggs and pussy willow branches](./images/easter-activities-417-land/happy-easter-sign.jpg)

## Easter cookie bouquet class

**Rustic Romance Vintage Marketplace**

Have you ever seen a beautiful Easter cookie and wished you knew how to make one? Julie's Cookie Jar has taught an Easter cookie bouquet class at Rustic Romance Vintage Marketplace. Past classes have run about two hours, with easy how-to instructions, hands-on practice and all the tools and sweets needed to make an Easter cookie bouquet.

Check with the marketplace for this year's class schedule.

## Easter day fun

**Thompson Flower Farm**

Thompson Flower Farm has hosted Easter day fun with music, vendors, food, games and an Easter egg hunt. Check the farm's social pages for this year's plans.

## Nixa Parks and Recreation egg hunt

**Nixa Parks and Recreation**

Nixa is a good place to spot the Easter Bunny and maybe get a picture with him. The Nixa Parks and Recreation egg hunt has been divided by age group so every kid has a fair chance at the eggs.

See [Nixa Parks and Recreation](https://www.nixaparks.com) for the current event calendar.

## Easter family fun day

**Renovation Church**

Renovation Church has welcomed everyone to a free Easter family fun day. Past events have featured crafts, a petting zoo, bucket train rides, an Easter egg hunt and more. Check the church's social pages for this year's event.

## Easter story time

**Art Zone**

Art Zone in Nixa has held an Easter-themed story time featuring "How to Catch the Easter Bunny," with a ceramic to glaze afterward. See [Art Zone's events](https://www.artzonenixa.com/events) for this year's lineup.

## All-day indoor egg hunt

**Discovery Center of Springfield**

The Discovery Center of Springfield hides eggs throughout the museum for an all-day indoor hunt. A recent hunt let kids trade their eggs for prizes and learn a little science along the way, and the egg hunt activities were free with general admission.

See the [Discovery Center's Easter page](https://www.discoverycenter.org/easter/) for the next date and current details.
