Why aren't more people in Springfield talking about Pizza House on Commercial? This place doesn't get the attention it deserves.

## When the Moon Hits Your Eye

Their pizza pie really is amore. They've been perfecting their craft since 1958.

One thing that sets their pizzas apart from other local pizza joints is the hand-rolled thin crust that every pizza is built on.

Hear me out, deep dish lovers. I was once a thick crust loyalist too. But one day I hesitantly decided to give Pizza House's thin crust a try, and I've never looked back. There's some kind of magic in that crust that makes it delicious even though it's thin.

## Stop On In

Pizza House is in a brick building at [312 E Commercial St](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Pizza+House/@37.229518,-93.2922277,17z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m5!3m4!1s0x87cf62f2c6668b8f:0x1af1e58bf8da5eca!8m2!3d37.229518!4d-93.290039).

That area of town is adorable. Across the street you'll spot the historic footbridge, and the C-Street Farmers Market sets up nearby in season. Check the market's schedule for days and times.

Step inside and you'll see a setup that looks the way you'd expect for a restaurant that opened in 1958: vintage signs, chairs covered in shiny teal and a jukebox in the corner. The staff greets you like an old friend, whether it's your first visit or you've been going since the 50s.

Leave yourself some time. Even if you only run in to grab a carry-out order, the warm atmosphere makes you want to stay awhile.

## My Recommendation

Pizza House offers all the standard toppings. I've tried quite a few combinations and have never been disappointed.

Everyone has their own favorite toppings, even if some of those preferences are very, very wrong (I'm looking at you, pineapple pizza people). Order whatever inspires you.

My biggest recommendation is to get over to Commercial Street and try their pizza soon.
