# Mahj Mondays at the Gillioz — thumbnail test

## Hook words

**SIT WITH US**

The title already says the game and the place. It does not say you are invited in. These three short words are the tap: a seat at the table, beginners included, no price and no extra promise. Stacked in yellow with a black stroke, they stay readable at 320 px wide.

## How it was made

1. Scene: Cursor GenerateImage, 16:9, no letters in the picture. Prompt:

   Photorealistic 16:9 YouTube thumbnail photograph, no text anywhere, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks, no signs. A close, phone-friendly crop inside a warm historic theatre gallery in downtown Springfield, Missouri at dusk in December. Left 42 percent of the frame is a simple dark empty wall in deep burgundy-brown shadow, completely uncluttered, reserved as negative space. On the right, three fictional adult friends sit close around a wooden table playing mahjong, faces large and sharp, laughing. All three look toward the empty left side. The person nearest the center points left into that empty space. Original fictional faces only, not celebrities or lookalikes. A few cream and jade mahjong tiles in the foreground. A tall window shows a snowy Midwest downtown evening: brick buildings, streetlamps, falling snow, cool blue night against amber lamps. Wool coats on chair backs. No marquee, no theatre sign, no readable writing, no brand marks, no Christmas characters. High contrast, one focal point.

2. Type: Pillow, Inter Bold, 156 px, fill RGB(255, 214, 20), 13 px stroke RGB(18, 6, 2). The words are drawn as the strings SIT, WITH, and US so they cannot come out misspelled. No other text was added.

3. Tiles: the generator drew pseudo-characters on the tile faces. A Pillow pass filled the darker interiors of the cream and green faces so those marks would not read as words. The only words on the image are the hook.

Output is 1280×720. `thumbnail.webp` is the delivery file (under 200 KB). `thumbnail-source.png` is the lossless composite.
